You are offline

This app cannot contact emergency services or send an SOS while offline. Do not wait for the app if someone is in immediate danger. Use your phone to call emergency services.

Call emergency number 112

Immediate safety steps

  • Flood: Move to higher ground. Stay out of floodwater, drains, and underpasses. Do not walk or drive through moving water.
  • Earthquake: Drop, cover, and hold on. Stay away from windows. After shaking stops, use stairs and avoid damaged buildings.
  • Fire or smoke: Leave by the nearest safe exit, stay low under smoke, and never use an elevator. Do not re-enter.
  • Injury: Call emergency services. Apply firm, continuous pressure to serious bleeding with a clean cloth.

Follow local authorities. Cached alerts, shelter availability, routes, and weather may be old or incomplete. Map tiles and live updates need an internet connection.
